# VårtJa

VårtJa is a Norwegian wedding platform that gives couples one place to share their wedding with their guests.

Couples can publish updates and photos, organize the wedding program, and manage households and guest access. Guests can follow the celebration, read practical information, and participate in comments and polls.

The platform includes a mobile-friendly wedding website and a Norwegian-language administration interface.

Built with Next.js, Payload CMS and PostgreSQL.
